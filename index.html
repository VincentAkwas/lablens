About LabLens Pro

LabLens Pro v3.0

A comprehensive web-based medical laboratory result interpreter built for MLS students, healthcare trainees, and clinical educators.

  • 12 comprehensive lab panels
  • 95+ individual analyte tests
  • Sex-specific reference ranges
  • Critical value flagging
  • 14 medical calculators
  • Batch processing mode
  • AI-powered clinical insights
  • Printable PDF reports
  • Offline capable — no server needed
Panels Included
  • CBC — Complete Blood Count (extended with differentials)
  • BMP / CMP — Metabolic Panels
  • LFT — Liver Function Tests
  • Lipid Panel — Full cholesterol panel
  • Thyroid — TSH, T3, T4, Anti-TPO
  • Coagulation — PT, PTT, INR, D-Dimer
  • ABG — Arterial Blood Gas
  • Urinalysis — Full urine panel
  • Iron Studies — Fe, TIBC, Ferritin, Sat%
  • Cardiac Markers — Troponin, CK-MB, BNP
  • Inflammatory — CRP, ESR, Procalcitonin
⚠️ IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER

This software is designed for EDUCATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY. It is NOT intended for clinical decision making, patient diagnosis, or treatment planning. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.


Reference ranges may vary between laboratories. All calculations and interpretations should be verified by a qualified medical professional before any educational or training application.


By using this software, you acknowledge it is an educational training tool only.
